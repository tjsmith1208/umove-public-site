1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you create an account and use the app:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to operate and improve U-Move:

Account management
Creating your account, signing you in, and keeping your profile up to date.
Job matching
Showing your posted job to available U-Movers, and showing available jobs to U-Movers in the area.
Notifications
Sending push alerts when a mover accepts your job, when a job status changes, or when a dispute is updated.
Reviews
Allowing clients to leave star ratings and written reviews after a job is completed, and displaying those reviews on mover profiles.
Dispute resolution
Investigating job disputes opened by clients or flagged by movers, and following up via email.
Support
Responding to questions, account issues, and safety reports sent to our support email.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Visible to other users: Job postings (description, addresses, photos, and move date) are visible to authenticated U-Movers browsing the app. Mover profiles (name, school, skills, badges, and reviews) are visible to authenticated clients browsing for movers. No user’s email address, phone number, or account credentials are shown to other users.

Firebase (Google): U-Move uses Firebase as its backend infrastructure, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore (database), Firebase Storage, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). Data stored in Firebase is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

U-Move does not collect:
  • Payment or financial information — U-Move does not process payments
  • Precise or background location — location is used only during active address entry
  • Contacts from your address book
  • Health or fitness data
  • Browsing history or search history outside the app
  • Any data used to track you across third-party apps or websites

U-Move does not engage in cross-app or cross-website tracking.

5. App Permissions

U-Move requests the following iOS permissions. Each permission is optional where noted and is only used for the stated purpose.

Permission When requested How it is used
Camera When a client chooses to take a photo during job posting Captures photos of items or spaces to attach to a job listing
Photo Library When a client chooses to upload an existing photo Selects photos from your library to attach to a job listing or set a profile picture
Location (When In Use) When the app is in the foreground and you are entering an address Assists with address auto-complete and displays nearby available jobs
Push Notifications After sign-up, the system will prompt for permission Sends alerts for job acceptances, status changes, and dispute updates

All permissions can be revoked at any time in your iPhone Settings → U-Move.

6. Payments

U-Move does not process or collect payments. The app displays quote estimates to help clients and movers understand expected costs before a job begins. All amounts shown are clearly labeled as estimates.

Payment is arranged directly between the client and the mover after the job is completed. No payment card data, banking information, Apple Pay, or in-app purchase integration is present in the app.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Completed job records are retained to support dispute resolution and review integrity. When you delete your account, your profile is flagged for removal from our database and your Firebase Authentication account is permanently deleted.

Anonymized usage and crash data collected by Firebase may be retained by Google per their own data retention policies.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time, directly inside the app:

  1. Open U-Move and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the prompt. Your profile is permanently deleted and you are signed out.

If the app asks you to sign out and sign back in before deleting, this is a security step required by Firebase. Sign out, sign back in, and try again.

You may also request account deletion by emailing support@umoveapp.com.

9. Children’s Privacy

U-Move is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact support@umoveapp.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify users via the app or by email before the changes take effect. Continued use of U-Move after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
