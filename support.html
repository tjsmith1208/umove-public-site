Frequently asked questions

How does U-Move work?

Clients post a job with a description, pickup and drop-off addresses, and preferred dates. U-Movers in the area browse open jobs and accept ones that fit their schedule. Once a mover accepts, both parties receive a notification and can coordinate for the job.

How are quote estimates calculated?

The app generates an instant estimate based on the number of movers needed, estimated hours, distance, and any optional add-ons (dolly, trailer, pressure washer, etc.). All amounts shown are estimates only.

Final payment is agreed upon directly between the client and the mover after the job is completed. U-Move does not process or collect any payments.

Does U-Move process payments?

No. U-Move does not collect or process payments of any kind. The app provides quote estimates to help both parties set expectations before a job begins. Payment is arranged directly between the client and the mover.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app and go to the Profile tab. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account. A confirmation prompt will appear — confirm to permanently delete your profile and sign out.

If the app asks you to sign out and sign back in first, that's a security step required before account deletion. Sign out, sign back in, then try again.

You can also request account deletion by emailing support@umoveapp.com.

What if I have a problem with a job?

You can open a dispute directly from the job detail screen in the app. Our support team reviews all disputes and will follow up via email. You can also email support@umoveapp.com directly to describe the issue.

Is U-Move available in my city?

U-Move is currently launching in select university towns and expanding from there. If U-Movers aren't yet active in your area, you can still post a job — we'll work to grow coverage in your market over time.

Email us at support@umoveapp.com if you'd like to know about specific cities or want to be notified when U-Move launches near you.

How do I sign up as a U-Mover?

Download the U-Move app and tap I'm a U-Mover on the opening screen. Create your account, fill in your profile details (school, skills, availability), and you'll be ready to browse and accept jobs in your area.

Is U-Move safe to use?

U-Move is designed for moving and labor services between consenting adults. All user accounts are tied to verified email addresses. Clients and movers can see each other's profiles and reviews before agreeing to a job.

If you have a safety concern about a specific job or user, contact us immediately at support@umoveapp.com. For emergencies, always call 911.

Can I change my role from Client to U-Mover (or vice versa)?

Your role is set when you create your account and cannot be changed in the app. If you need a different role, email us at support@umoveapp.com and we can assist.
