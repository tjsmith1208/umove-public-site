1. About U-Move

U-Move is a peer-to-peer marketplace that connects clients who need moving or labor assistance (“Clients”) with individuals who provide those services (“U-Movers”). U-Move is a platform only — we do not employ U-Movers, direct the manner of their work, or guarantee job availability for either party.

U-Movers are independent individuals, not employees or agents of U-Move. U-Move is not a moving company and does not provide moving services directly.

4. Client Rules

As a Client, you agree to:

  • Post only accurate, good-faith job descriptions — including honest address information, realistic scope, and correct move dates.
  • Not post jobs for illegal activities or services U-Move does not support.
  • Confirm job completion honestly. Marking a job complete when it has not been performed is a violation of these Terms.
  • Arrange payment directly with your U-Mover after the job is completed. U-Move does not process payments on your behalf.
  • Only open a dispute if you have a genuine, good-faith concern about the service provided.

5. U-Mover Rules

As a U-Mover, you agree to:

  • Only accept jobs you are genuinely able and willing to complete.
  • Arrive on time and perform the work as described in the job posting.
  • Not misrepresent your skills, physical capabilities, or equipment availability.
  • Mark job status transitions (start, pending completion) accurately and promptly.
  • Arrange payment directly with the Client after the job is completed.
  • Not solicit Clients off-platform or request payment through the app in ways that circumvent the agreed quote.

U-Move is not responsible for payment disputes, non-payment, or disagreements about final amounts between Clients and U-Movers.
