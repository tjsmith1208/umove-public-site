About U-Move

U-Move is a peer-to-peer marketplace connecting people who need moving, hauling, or labor help with local college students who are ready to work.

Clients post a job in minutes, get an instant quote estimate, and track their job from request to completion. U-Movers browse open jobs, accept work that fits their schedule, and build a reputation through verified reviews and badges.

U-Move does not process or collect payments. All quote amounts are estimates. Payment is arranged directly between the client and the mover after the job.

U-Move is not an emergency service. For emergencies, always call 911. For support, contact support@umoveapp.com.
